**LEDGERA** is an independent validator team focused on operating reliable and secure infrastructure for decentralized networks — and on giving delegators the same visibility into their stake that operators have.

Ledgera started as **Delega Pro**, a monitoring system we built for our own validators in the Web3 Foundation's 1KV and Decentralized Nodes programme on Polkadot and Kusama. Per-era performance reports, commission change alerts and infrastructure drift detection kept our nodes competitive — and we realized delegators needed the same transparency.

In 2025 we expanded to Celestia, Starknet, Dymension, Lava and Nibiru. Moving from Polkadot to multi-chain is not a pivot — it's the same work, applied to more networks.

---

## Networks

LEDGERA runs validators across several ecosystems: Cosmos SDK chains, Ethereum and Starknet. We are selective about where we participate, and we only validate networks we believe in.

The tooling we use to run our own nodes is published openly. **[node-scripts](https://github.com/LedgeraLedgera/node-scripts)** holds our one-command installers: verified release binaries, state sync, systemd and tools for status checks and upgrades. We write and maintain these scripts for the networks we support, and add each new network as we join it.

## Delegator Transparency

**[proledgera.com/transparency](https://proledgera.com/transparency/)** — paste any wallet address and get a report on what your stake is actually doing: idle rewards, liquid balance that isn't staked, effective net APR, stake concentration and commission drag across your validators.

What we track:

- **Commission + history** — every rate change, with the date it moved  
- **Trust score** — open formula, printed, not a black box  
- **Governance votes** — how each validator voted vs. you  
- **Uptime by proposals** — post-Matcha metric, not just signatures  
- **Telegram alerts** — quiet commission raises, jailing, stake drops  

Live example: a read-only demo wallet on Celestia (21M TIA staked) — figures are estimates from on-chain data.

Source: [`celestia-transparency`](https://github.com/LedgeraLedgera/celestia-transparency)

## Infrastructure

Our infrastructure is designed with stability and control in mind.

- dedicated bare-metal servers in reliable data centers  
- isolated validator environments  
- HSM-based key management and multi-layer firewall protection  
- automated backups and recovery workflows  
- 24/7 monitoring with real-time alerting and failover  

Where applicable, infrastructure is distributed to reduce single points of failure.

---

## Operations

Validator operations are maintained with a focus on consistency and predictability.

- high uptime participation in consensus  
- careful validator configuration management  
- controlled rollout of updates and proactive maintenance  
- performance monitoring and tuning  
- incident response procedures  

We avoid aggressive configurations in favor of stable and reproducible behavior.

---

## Security Approach

Security is integrated into all layers of operations.

- strict access control policies  
- isolated signing environments  
- monitoring for abnormal validator behavior  
- procedures to prevent double-signing and downtime penalties  

Operational safety takes priority over short-term performance gains.

---

## Principles

- infrastructure must be predictable and controlled  
- validator reliability is more important than short-term optimization  
- participation in networks is a long-term commitment  
- transparency builds trust with delegators
